Getting started

Create your account once, and FarmGo shows you the screens for your role. The app works in English and Kiswahili.

Create an account

Pick what describes you best. Farmers and households sign up with a phone number; hotels, restaurants and youth enterprises use an email address.

  1. Open the app and tap Get Started. Use the EN SW switch at the top to choose your language first.
  2. Choose Hotel / Restaurant, Farmer, Youth Enterprise or Individual / Household.
  3. With a phone number, tap Send code and type the 6-digit code from the SMS. With email, fill in your name, email and a password, then tap Create account.
  4. Tick I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy.
  5. Finish the short setup. Farmers add their county, M-Pesa number for payments and their farm, pinned on the map. Businesses add the business name, type, phone and KRA PIN. Households add where to deliver. Then tap Finish setup.
You need to be 18 or older to sign up. At the end of setup, tap Turn on notifications so you never miss an order.

Sign in

Use the same way you signed up.

  1. On the welcome screen, tap Login.
  2. Phone accounts: choose Phone, enter your number and tap Send code, then type the code and tap Verify. If it does not arrive, wait for Resend code.
  3. Email accounts: choose Email, enter your email and password, and tap Sign in.
Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? and then Send reset link. The link in the email works for one hour.

Buyers

For hotels, restaurants, caterers and households. Shop fresh produce straight from farms, or tell farmers what you will need before they harvest.

Your tabs

HomeOrdersMessagesProfile

Find produce

Everything on FarmGo comes from a named farm, so you always know where your food grows.

  1. On Home, type into Search for products.... Kiswahili names work too, so "nyanya" finds tomatoes.
  2. Or tap a tile under Categories, such as Vegetables or Fruits, and narrow it with the chips at the top.
  3. Scroll to Featured Farmers and Fresh near Nairobi to buy from farms close to you.
  4. Tap Add to Cart straight from the list, or open the item to see more.

Check a product and its farm

The product page shows the price per unit, the grade, when the harvest is ready and how much is left.

  1. Read the price, grade and the Harvest ready from date.
  2. Tap the farm card to see the farmer: their rating, orders delivered and how often their produce passes the quality check.
  3. Set the amount with the minus and plus buttons, then tap Add to Cart. The message that appears offers View cart.
  4. Tap the heart to save the item to your favorites for next time.

Check out and pay

One cart can hold produce from several farms. Each farm gets its own order, all placed and paid for together.

  1. Open the cart, check Subtotal, Delivery Fee and Total, and tap Proceed to Checkout.
  2. Under Delivery Details, confirm the address and pick a Preferred Delivery Time. Add Notes for the driver if they need a gate code or a name to ask for.
  3. Under Payment Method, choose M-Pesa and check the M-Pesa number. Business accounts on credit terms can choose Add to invoice.
  4. Tap Pay KES with the amount. A prompt arrives on your phone: enter your M-Pesa PIN.
  5. When you see Payment received, you are done. Tap Track my orders to follow it.
Payment did not go through? Your order is saved. On the screen that says the payment was not completed, check the M-Pesa number and tap Try again, or tap Pay later and pay from Orders.

Follow and track an order

Orders update by themselves. You do not need to refresh.

  1. Open the Orders tab. Active shows orders on their way; Past shows the finished ones.
  2. Open an order to see its steps: Order placed, At the farm, On the way, Delivered.
  3. Once a driver has the order, tap Track order to see the map, your driver, and buttons to call or message them.

Message the farmer

Each order has its own conversation, so questions about harvest, packing or delivery stay with that order.

  1. Open the order and tap Message farmer.
  2. Type in Write a message and send. Tap the photo button to share a picture.
  3. All your conversations are in the Messages tab. A badge shows unread messages.

Confirm receipt, rate or report a problem

Confirming tells FarmGo the delivery is right, and that releases the farmer's payment.

  1. When the order says Delivered, check the produce.
  2. All good? Tap Confirm receipt, then Yes, all good.
  3. Tap Rate this order, choose the stars, add a comment if you like and tap Submit rating.
  4. Something wrong? Tap Report a problem, choose what went wrong, describe what happened, add photos and tap Send report.
  5. To buy the same again, open a past order and tap Order again. Items go back in your cart at today's price.
Report problems within 48 hours of delivery. While a report is open, the farmer is not paid until it is sorted out. Need to cancel? If the order can still be cancelled, you will see Cancel order on it, and any payment goes back to M-Pesa.

Post a requirement

Tell farmers what you will need before they harvest. FarmGo proposes matches from nearby farms, and you accept the ones you want.

  1. On Home, tap the banner that says Tell farmers what you need.
  2. Choose the Produce, the Quantity, the Lowest grade you accept and, if you want, the Most you will pay.
  3. Pick the date it is Needed by, then how often: Just once, Every week, Every 2 weeks or Every month. The form previews your next delivery dates.
  4. Tap Post requirement.
  5. When farmers are matched, open Matches and tap Accept. Once both sides accept, the order is created.
Open a requirement any time to Pause, Resume or Close requirement.

Pay an invoice

For business accounts that FarmGo has approved for credit terms. Orders added to an invoice are billed weekly.

  1. Open Invoices from your Home screen or a notification.
  2. Open an invoice to see each order on it. Its status is Due, Part paid, Paid or Overdue.
  3. Tap Pay KES by M-Pesa, then Send M-Pesa prompt, and enter your PIN.
You can also pay by bank transfer quoting the invoice number. While an invoice is overdue, new orders may need payment up front.

Shopping as a household

Households use the same shop, cart and tracking as businesses, with two differences.

  1. Households always pay in advance by M-Pesa. Credit terms and invoices are not available.
  2. Your delivery area shows at the top of Home. Change it, or add up to 20 addresses, under Profile, Delivery addresses.

Farmers

List what you grow, see what buyers need, confirm orders and get paid to M-Pesa. Everything is built to work one-handed, in English or Kiswahili.

Your tabs

HomeOrdersSellMessagesProfile

Your farmer home

Home shows what needs you today, so you can act in a few taps.

  1. The cards at the top count your listings, orders and sales.
  2. Needs your action lists new orders. Tap Confirm order right there.
  3. Below it, My Products shows your live listings. Tap View All to manage them.

List produce for sale

Six short steps, from choosing the farm to publishing. You can list produce that is still growing.

  1. Tap the green Sell button in the middle of your tab bar.
  2. Answer Which farm is this from? and What are you selling?, then tap Continue.
  3. Enter the Quantity you can supply, your Price per unit and the Grade. Not sure of the price? Tap Use the market price.
  4. Set Ready from and Available until, and add photos of your produce.
  5. Check everything and tap Publish listing. You will see Your listing is live. Not ready yet? Tap Save as paused instead.

Manage your listings

Keep your stock and prices right so buyers only order what you can deliver.

  1. Open your listings from Home. They are grouped as Live, Upcoming and Closed.
  2. Open one to Edit listing, Pause it, or Close listing.
  3. A closed listing can go back on sale with Put back on the market.
Pausing a listing stops new orders. Orders already placed are not affected.

Confirm an order and mark the harvest ready

Every order shows exactly what you will receive after the FarmGo fee.

  1. A new order shows as New order. Open it and tap Confirm order. The buyer is told straight away.
  2. Harvest and pack the produce for the delivery date shown.
  3. When it is ready, tap Mark ready. A QA officer comes to inspect it at the farm gate.
  4. Check Your payout on the order: the produce value, the FarmGo fee, and You receive.
Marked a harvest ready by mistake? Open the listing and tap Undo harvest ready. This works only until the QA officer has started the inspection.

See what buyers need

Hotels and restaurants post what they will need in the coming weeks. Grow and list against real demand.

  1. Open What buyers need from your Home shortcuts.
  2. Filter by county, and tap Matches my produce to see only what you grow.
  3. Tap List this produce to open Sell with the details filled in.
  4. When FarmGo matches you to a buyer, open Matches and tap Accept or Decline.

Track your earnings

See every shilling: what you have been paid, what is on the way and what is still held.

  1. Open Earnings from Home.
  2. Paid to you is money already in your M-Pesa. Being sent is on its way. Held for you is waiting for the buyer to confirm delivery.
  3. Tap any payout to see the breakdown and the M-Pesa receipt.
  4. Payouts go to the number shown. Tap Edit next to it to change it.
You are paid when the buyer confirms delivery, or 48 hours after delivery. If a payout fails, FarmGo retries automatically. If a buyer reports a problem, that payout is on hold until it is resolved.

Buy green inputs

Compost, organic fertilizer and seedlings from youth enterprises near you.

  1. Open Green inputs from Home and browse the Shop.
  2. Open a product, tap Order now, choose the quantity and pay with M-Pesa.
  3. Follow it under My orders. When it arrives, tap I received it.

Register a farmer

The farmer gets an SMS to confirm their number. Orders and payments are confirmed to them by SMS too.

  1. Tap Add farmer in the middle of your tab bar.
  2. Under About the farmer, fill in the full name as on their ID, phone number, county and date of birth. Choose their Language for SMS.
  3. Under Payments, keep Pay to the same number on M-Pesa, or enter a different M-Pesa number.
  4. Turn on Add their farm now and Pin the farm on the map.
  5. Tap Register farmer.
Farmers must be 18 or older. Payouts start once the farmer's ID is verified.

Look after your farmers

Your Farmers tab lists everyone you have registered.

  1. Search by name, phone or farm, or filter by No farm yet and ID pending.
  2. Open a farmer to Add farm and then Save farm.
  3. Tap List produce for this farm to sell on their behalf, using the same steps as a farmer.
  4. Their orders appear in your Orders tab, where you can Confirm order and Mark ready for them.

QA officers

Inspect produce at the farm gate. Your result decides what the buyer pays for and what the farmer is paid.

Your tabs

TasksHistoryProfile

Choose an inspection

Inspections lists every order whose harvest is ready, with the farm, produce and delivery date.

  1. Filter by county with the chips at the top. Cards show Waiting or Overdue.
  2. Use the call and directions buttons to reach the farmer.
  3. At the farm, tap Start inspection. A half-finished one shows Continue.

Inspect an order

Grade each item and record how much you accept.

  1. Answer Where are you inspecting?: Farm gate, Collection centre or Buyer door.
  2. For each item, choose Pass or Fail, set the Grade, and the amounts Accepted and Rejected.
  3. If anything is rejected, say why and add at least one photo. Work through the Checklist.
  4. Tap Submit inspection. If items fail, the button reads Submit with failures and asks you to confirm.
Only accepted produce is paid for, and an inspection cannot be undone. If you accept part of an order, the buyer is refunded for the rest. If the whole order fails, the buyer gets a full refund.

Drivers

Run your route: collect from farms, deliver to buyers, and keep track of every crate.

Your tabs

TodayScanRoutesProfile

Start your route

Today shows the routes dispatch has given you, with every stop in order.

  1. On Today, tap Open route.
  2. Tap Start route. Buyers can follow your location until the last stop is done.
  3. The route shows Sharing live location while you drive.
No route yet? Tap Check again. Start the route before working on its stops.

Pick up and deliver

At each stop, scan the crates so FarmGo knows exactly what moved where.

  1. Open the stop and tap Navigate. When you arrive, tap I am here.
  2. Tap Scan crates and scan each crate as you load or unload it. If a code will not scan, type it under Or type the code.
  3. At a farm, tap Confirm pickup.
  4. At a buyer, add a photo or a signature under Proof of delivery, fill in Received by, and tap Confirm delivery.
The buyer is not charged for crates you do not scan. Scan empty crates the buyer returns too.

When a stop cannot be completed

Sometimes a gate is closed or nobody answers. Record it so the buyer is told.

  1. On the stop, tap Cannot complete this stop.
  2. Choose what went wrong, for example Closed when I arrived or Could not reach anyone.
  3. Tap Mark as failed. The order leaves your route and the buyer is told. This cannot be undone.
  4. To check any crate, use Scan in the tab bar. You can mark it Mark returned or Mark lost.

Add a product

Good photos and an honest description sell better.

  1. On your products, tap Add product.
  2. Add photos, the Product name, Category, Description, price per unit and how many are In stock.
  3. Say Where farmers collect or you deliver, then tap Publish product.
  4. Out of stock for a while? Open the product and tap Hide from farmers.

Handle input orders

Orders move through New, In progress and Done.

  1. Open a new order and tap Accept or Decline.
  2. Once the farmer has paid, tap Mark dispatched. Until then the order says it is waiting for the farmer to pay.
  3. When it arrives, tap Mark delivered.
Farmers pay by M-Pesa when they order. The money is sent to you, less the FarmGo fee, when they confirm delivery.

Overview and people

Overview counts what needs attention, with a link from each number straight to the work.

  1. Use View orders, Resolve disputes, Retry payouts and Review ID checks to jump in.
  2. In People, open a user to Change role, Suspend account, or Approve or Reject an ID check.
  3. Open an organization to set its Payment terms, Credit limit and Verified buyer status.
  4. To see the app as someone else, use View as another user. Tap Back to my account when done.
While viewing as another user, money actions are blocked. The view ends by itself after 30 minutes.

Resolve disputes and payouts

Money keeps payouts, payments, invoices and disputes in one place.

  1. Open Disputes, pick one and tap Mark under review.
  2. Tap Resolve dispute and choose Refund the buyer with an amount, or No refund. Write the decision note.
  3. Under Payouts, filter Failed and tap Retry.
  4. For bank transfers or cash, use Record payment with the order code or invoice number.

Plan deliveries

Routes batch orders that passed the quality check into one trip.

  1. In Logistics, choose the day and tap Build routes.
  2. Open a route and Assign driver.
  3. Under Crates, use Register crates to add new reusable crates.

Payments and statuses

How money moves, and what each label on an order means.

Ways to pay

What you see at checkout depends on your account.

  • M-PesaA prompt arrives on your phone; enter your PIN and the screen updates by itself. Everyone can use it.
  • Add to invoiceOnly for business accounts approved for credit terms. Billed weekly, due 7, 14 or 30 days after delivery.
  • Card / Bank TransferShown when it is available. You finish paying on a secure page.
  • Bank or cashRecorded by FarmGo staff for buyers who paid outside M-Pesa.

When farmers are paid

Payouts go to the farmer's M-Pesa number.

  • Held for youThe buyer has paid. The money waits until delivery is confirmed.
  • ProcessingConfirmed, or 48 hours passed after delivery. The payout is being sent.
  • PaidIn your M-Pesa, with the receipt on the payout.
  • On holdA problem was reported. Paid once it is resolved.
  • FailedFarmGo retries automatically. Check your M-Pesa number in Earnings.

Order statuses

Buyers and farmers see the same order with slightly different words where it helps.

  • Waiting for farmerPlaced and paid. Farmers see this as New order.
  • ConfirmedThe farmer accepted it. Farmers see To harvest.
  • Quality checkHarvested and waiting for a QA officer.
  • Ready for pickupPassed the check and waiting for the driver.
  • Failed quality checkRejected at inspection. The buyer is not charged for it.
  • On the wayWith the driver.
  • DeliveredArrived. The buyer confirms receipt or reports a problem within 48 hours.
  • Problem reportedFarmGo is looking into it. Most reports are resolved within a day.
  • CompleteDelivered, confirmed and the farmer is paid. Farmers see Paid.
  • RefundedThe money went back to the buyer.
  • CancelledStopped before delivery. Any payment goes back to M-Pesa.

Account and help

Your profile, language, notifications and where to get help.

Profile, language and notifications

Everything about your account lives in the Profile tab.

  1. Change language under Preferences, Language: English or Kiswahili. The whole app switches at once.
  2. Open Notification settings to choose app, SMS and email messages, and set Quiet hours for the night.
  3. Use Edit profile to update your details, and Security to change your password or sign out other devices.
  4. Choose Appearance: Auto, Light or Dark.
Important messages about payments and deliveries always come by SMS, even during quiet hours.

Get help

Answers to common questions are inside the app, sorted by topic.

  1. Go to Profile, then Help.
  2. Pick a topic: Ordering, Payments, Delivery or Account, and tap a question to open the answer.
  3. Still stuck? Ask your FarmGo field agent or account manager.

Quick answers

I did not get the SMS code.

Check the number, wait for Resend code, and make sure your phone has signal. You can also tap Change number to fix a typo.

Can I use the app in Kiswahili?

Yes. Go to Profile, Preferences, Language and choose Kiswahili. Before you sign in, use the SW switch at the top of the welcome screen.

Why is my order still waiting for the farmer?

The farmer has not confirmed it yet. You can message them from the order with Message farmer.

Part of my order was rejected at the quality check. Do I pay for it?

No. Only accepted produce is paid for. The rest is refunded to your M-Pesa automatically.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from Profile, Delete account. It is refused while you still have open orders, or while payouts are on their way to you. Finish those first.
